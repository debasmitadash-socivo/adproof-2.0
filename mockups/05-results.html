What's working

  • Psychology cues in the copy (+1.47) — emotional appeal, scarcity, social proof and relevance all firing on the same creative.
  • Visual scores (+1.04) — strong attention capture, clean focal point, low edge clutter.

What's holding it back

Recommendations

  • The p10 downside still lands at 2.10× — you can scale budget. Sensitivity suggests near-linear returns up to ~$40k spend.
