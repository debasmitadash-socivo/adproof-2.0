Screen 01

Sign in / sign up

Sunset-gradient hero with the brand pitch ("Score every ad before you ship it."), Google / Apple / email sign-in on the right.
Screen 02

Onboarding · Company profile

Paste a free-text company description; we parse it into a structured profile (industry, business model, category, price position, tone) and surface it live in a gradient card.
Screen 03

Dashboard

Sidebar nav · workspace switcher · "Good evening, Debs." in display italic · gradient CTA card · four tinted metric cards · recent-analyses table with status pills · saved audiences · recommended next moves.
Screen 04

New analysis · 5-step wizard

The big one. Step 3 (Audience) ships with platform-native targeting filters — exactly mirroring Meta Ads Manager's Detailed Targeting (Interests · Behaviours · Demographics · Life events), Custom & Lookalike audiences. Toggle to Google / LinkedIn / TikTok and the filter set adapts. Live reach estimate in the right rail.
Screen 05 · the payoff

Forecast · interactive scorecard

Big italic ROAS number on a fresh-emerald gradient hero · glass-effect verdict grade · gradient-striped metric tiles · six tabbed live Plotly charts in brand palette · italic "Why this ad performs" narrative with what's-working / what's-holding-it-back / recommendations · sticky right-rail Edit & re-run panel so you can tweak budget / format / audience without leaving the page. Plus Duplicate / Export PDF / Share / Compare-with-other-creatives.
Design language locked in this revision: warm cream paper background · sunset gradient (coral → magenta → violet) · fresh-emerald and lime accents · Instrument Serif italic for display headlines · Bricolage Grotesque for section titles · Inter for body · JetBrains Mono for numbers. Status pills, gradient buttons, gradient-text accents on key headlines.
Platform-native filters confirmed in Step 4: Meta Ads Manager layout (Age slider · Gender · Location chips · Detailed Targeting grouped into Interests / Behaviours / Demographics → Life events · Connection & Custom / Lookalike). Same wizard surfaces Google-Ads-native filters (affinity, in-market, life events, household income) when Google is picked, and LinkedIn-native filters (job title · function · seniority · company size · industry) when LinkedIn is picked.
